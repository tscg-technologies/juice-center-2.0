About Juice Center

Fresh ingredients, handcrafted recipes, and an uncompromising commitment to hygienic refreshment.

Beverage Philosophy

Pure, Natural Refreshment in Every Glass

At Juice Center, we believe that true refreshment comes from nature itself. Founded on the principle of simplicity and natural vitality, our juice center focuses on serving high-quality cold-pressed juices, velvety shakes, wholesome smoothies, and artisanal mocktails.

Whether you are grabbing a morning orange juice before lectures or work, recharging after a workout, or relaxing with loved ones over a tall glass of Royal Falooda, Juice Center provides a welcoming haven of crisp, invigorating taste.

Our Standards

The Juice Center Difference

Every step—from sourcing whole fresh fruits to final blending—is guided by four essential standards.

🍎

Fresh Fruit Selection

We inspect whole seasonal fruits daily. Only mature, naturally sweet oranges, watermelons, mangoes, and pomegranates make it to our counters.

⚙️

Fresh Cold-Pressed Extraction

Juices are extracted on demand using gentle cold-press techniques that minimize heat buildup and preserve natural flavours.

🧼

Hygiene & Cleanliness

We maintain spotless preparation stations, sanitized stainless steel juicing components, filtered water, and food-grade glassware.

🍹

Taste & Variety

Over 25+ curated recipes ranging from zero-sugar healthy fruit cleanses to indulgent chocolate shakes and multi-layer faloodas.
